My Story

Blending strategy
with compassion

Wendy McLarty is a licensed social worker turned HR executive serving as Vice President of Human Resources — bringing a rare combination of psychological insight and business acumen to organizational leadership.

With over 19 years in social services and 14 years in HR management, she has established herself as a distinctive voice in workplace culture transformation — particularly as a female executive in male-dominated industries.

She specializes in Leadership Training, Workplace Culture Development, Team Building, and Crisis Management.

A wife, mother of three, and grandmother, she brings authentic understanding of what it means to balance professional ambition with personal growth.
